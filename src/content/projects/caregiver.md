Student carers are largely invisible to the institutions they study at. The project started from
research rather than from a product idea, and the hardware exists because self-reported mood is a
lagging indicator — HRV gives a signal before someone recognises they are struggling.

User testing validated both the usability and the premise: the tool was judged useful by the
people it was designed for, which for a project about wellbeing mattered more than any technical
measure.
